The people named on the reviews.

Grand Detailing is a car detailing service. The public Google listing has an address, a phone number, hours, two reviews, and seven photos. This page sticks to that.

The address is 78 Stagsden Road, Bedford MK43 8PU. That part of Stagsden Road is in Bromham. Bedford is the postal town. The plus code on the listing is 4FV8+QF Bedford.

Google’s category on the listing is “Car detailing service”. There is no website link and no email address on it. The phone number is 07496 014846.

Ryan Porte’s review names Ollie and Luke. They turned up on time and cleaned both of his vans to a high standard, in the rain. He then booked a regular clean.

Ollie Neal wrote that his vehicle was detailed inside and out, and that the service and the result surprised him. The owner replied on Google in 2023: “Thanks for leaving a review we're glad you like the final results!” The reply is not signed with a name.

Hours on the listing are 9am to 6pm, every day. Google also lists a wheelchair-accessible car park. If you need a certain space, say so when you call.

This site does not add a year the business opened, a list of products, or a set of prices. None of those are on the listing. If you need a price, phone them.
