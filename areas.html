Bedford, and the roads around the base.

The pin is on Stagsden Road in Bromham. Distances below are from that pin to the centre of each place, measured in a straight line. They are not a promise of travel. The listing has no mile limit. Call and name the road.

Bromham

The base is here

78 Stagsden Road sits in Bromham. Google prints the postal town, which is Bedford, and the postcode MK43 8PU. The plus code on the listing is 4FV8+QF Bedford. If the vehicle is on a Bromham road, you are as close as it gets.

Bedford

About 3 miles to the town centre

St Paul’s Square is about 3 miles east of the base. The listing uses Bedford as the town name. Grand Detailing has not published a map of streets they will travel. Ring and name the road.

Biddenham

About 1 mile

Biddenham is the next village towards Bedford, about 1 mile from the base. It is a short hop along the west side of town.

Great Denham

About 1.5 miles

Great Denham is about 1.5 miles south of the base. Name the street when you call, the same as anywhere else.

Oakley

About 2 miles

Oakley is about 2 miles north of the base. It is one of the nearer villages on this side of Bedford.

Stagsden

About 2 miles

The road is named for Stagsden, which is about 2 miles west. The business address is the Bromham end of Stagsden Road, not the village itself.

Stevington

About 2 miles

Stevington is about 2 miles north-west. It is close enough to ask about. It is not listed as a fixed stop.

Kempston

About 2.5 miles

Kempston is about 2.5 miles south-east, on the far side of the Great Ouse from Bromham. Call and check the day.

Clapham

About 3 miles

Clapham, the Bedford one, is about 3 miles north-east. This is not Clapham in London.

Turvey

About 3 miles

Turvey is about 3 miles west, further out along the same side of the county. Ask before you assume they can reach you that week.

Pavenham

About 3 miles

Pavenham is about 3 miles north. Same rule: call, give the road, and let them say yes or no.

Milton Keynes is about 12 miles away. It is not named on the listing. Do not assume they travel that far.
