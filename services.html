What customers have actually booked.

Google lets a reviewer tag the jobs from a visit. These are the tags on the two Grand Detailing reviews, plus the van work and the regular clean Ryan Porte wrote about. Call for a price. None are published.

Full body wash

Both Google reviews tag a full body wash. That is a wash of the outside of the vehicle, rather than a quick splash on the front. Ryan Porte and Ollie Neal each tagged it. When you call, say how big the vehicle is.

Interior and exterior detailing

Ollie Neal wrote that the inside and the outside of his vehicle were detailed. Ryan Porte tagged auto detailing on the visit for his vans. Ask what is included when you call. The listing has no set menu.

Car waxing

Ryan Porte tagged car waxing. Wax goes on the paint after the wash. The listing does not name the product. Ask which wax they use, and whether it suits your paint.

Wheel washing

Ryan Porte tagged wheel washing. One photo on the profile is a close shot of a clean black wheel and arch on a grey Range Rover. Call if the wheels are very dirty, or if you want them taken off the car. Taking wheels off is not listed as a separate job.

Interior vacuuming

Ryan Porte tagged auto interior vacuuming, which means the inside was vacuumed. A photo from the same summer shows the driver’s seat of a Kia. Mention child seats, dog hair, or a van floor when you call.

Seat shampooing

Ryan Porte tagged seat shampooing. The seats were shampooed, not just wiped. Cloth and leather need different care, so say what the seats are made of when you call.

Interior scenting

Ryan Porte tagged interior scenting. That is a scent in the cabin after the clean. If anyone who uses the car reacts to fragrance, say so and ask them to leave it out.

Van cleaning

Ryan Porte booked two vans. He wrote that both were cleaned to a high standard, even in the rain. One photo shows a Ford van covered in wash foam. Tell them if the van has a roof rack, tools, or a bulkhead.

Regular cleans

After the first visit, Ryan Porte wrote that he was booked in for a regular clean. The listing does not say how often that repeat happens. Ask what they can do again, and how far ahead they book.
